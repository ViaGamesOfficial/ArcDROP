02 Pod entries

Footprints are drawn on a 3-wide column, top-left anchored, unrotated. Dashed cells are enclosed gaps the pod does not occupy — they remain live array space.

Arrest PodViator1 cell · single

Manages controlled descent from any elevation. Terrain flagged as impassable is cleared for transit while equipped.

Cost 15 D · 10 CEffort 40Crafts/mission ~8

The only pod that fits leftover single cells. Its real value is larger than one cell suggests — it is what makes an enclosed pocket usable. Drives the Ledge Gate pass; there is no fall damage in the game either way, only terrain you can or cannot cross.

Sift PodIllumen2 cells · vertical domino

Broadens the operative's acquisition envelope. Retrievable material registers at extended range within boundary.

Cost 20 D · 15 CEffort 57Crafts/mission ~6

Implementation is an expanded interaction sphere on pickup actors — no drone, no world actor. Boundary-gated like every other benefit.

Brand PodIllumen2 cells · vertical domino

Munitions carry a persistent optical tag. Any hostile struck remains marked until neutralized.

Cost 25 D · 20 C · 20 FluxEffort 76Crafts/mission ~5

Marking is earned by landing a shot rather than granted outright. No occlusion pass in this version — the tag is not visible through terrain. Reserve that for a higher-tier variant.

Shot Type · TapArbiter2 cells · vertical domino

Single discharge per input. Higher yield per round, paced to the operative's timing.

Cost 20 C · 15 DEffort 60Crafts/mission ~6

Shot Types are unique-equip: one at a time until higher-level variants unlock a second and third weapon slot. Naming describes the operative's input, not the mechanism.

Shot Type · SustainViator3 cells · straight column

Continuous discharge while input is held. Reduced yield per round, compensated by volume.

Cost 30 C · 15 B · 40 FluxEffort 145Crafts/mission ~2–3

Three cells, but a polite three — a straight column leaves the neighbouring columns whole.

Shot Type · BlastViator5 cells · L, full column + kick

Wide-spectrum discharge. Coherent to extreme range, with a long cycle between shots.

Cost 5 A · 20 B · 10 C · 120 FluxEffort 209Crafts/mission ~2

The inversion: a shotgun that keeps coherence at range rather than losing it. Slow cycle and a hostile footprint are the whole downside — the foot kick out of the column is what breaks a neighbour's build.

Petrichor PodIllumen3 cells · L

Rectifies ambient storm discharge into usable current. AUX regeneration is elevated for the duration of active precipitation.

Cost 25 C · 20 B · 60 FluxEffort 165Crafts/mission ~2

Conditional power at an awkward shape. Depends on the Milestone 3 Weather System — first pod to make weather mechanical rather than atmospheric. Ties naturally into the Supercharged loop.

Black Box PodArbiter2 cells · diagonal, non-contiguous

Hardened single-cell vault, rated for total suit loss. One Field Cache slot is recovered intact after catastrophic failure.

Cost 20 C · 10 B · 50 FluxEffort 100 (baseline)Crafts/mission 3–4

Existing pod, folded into this spec as the cost baseline. Two cells that fragment the array — you pay in geometry rather than volume.

Tether PodArbiter4 cells · T

Severs the Shell on catastrophic suit failure and throws it clear to the last Gravity Well, preventing hostile capture of the array. The Field Cache does not survive transit.

Cost 10 A · 25 B · 250 FluxEffort 300 (3× baseline)Crafts/mission 1–2

Narrow insurance against one catastrophe: the invasion. Burns out on use; the spent husk holds its slots until you get home. Not standard issue because each unit is built from already-inverted Flux — every Tether costs a charge someone has already spent. Center-column T is deliberately poisonous to neighbours.

Ballast PodIllumen7 cells · I-beam

Mass-heavy field amplifier. Extends the Shell's boundary projection well past standard tolerance.

Cost 8 A · 40 B · 200 FluxEffort 347Crafts/mission ~1

Seven of twelve cells. The two side gaps are single-cell pockets — Arrest fits, nothing else does. This is the pod that turns the array into a one-decision build.

Signal PodUnsanctioned6 cells · arch with enclosed pocket

An unlicensed channel, patched into frequencies Command retired years ago. Terminal access grants contracts nobody files a report on — paid in full, provided you stay in range.

Cost 12 A · 20 B · 30 C · 400 FluxEffort 380Crafts/mission ~1

Spawns a Shell interior room with its own contract terminal. Jobs are accepted and paid only while the pod is equipped and the operative is inside the boundary — it is a content dispenser that argues for anchored play. Fiction: a rogue Illumen running a pirate broadcast through dimensional channels. The enclosed center cell is a real gap, not a socket; it just happens to fit almost nothing.

05 Effort vs. footprint

Cell count and cost should track loosely but not lock together — shape is its own tax. Black Box sits high for its two cells because it fragments; Blast sits below the line because its cost is paid in geometry.
