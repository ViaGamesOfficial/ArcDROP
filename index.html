NLOC file 01
Briefing

Reality split. You clean it up.

A phenomenon classified as a Divergence Event fractured the Prime Dimension into offshoots. Those Arc Dimensions grow, mutate and weigh on the reality we live in. The NonLinear Operatives Command works in total secrecy to collapse every one of them.

Viator Corps

Breach and stabilize. Combat specialists who go in first and hold the line inside hostile dimensions.

Illumen Division

Analyze and sequence. The scientists who track anomalies and plan how each dimension comes down.

Arbiter Class

Oversight and enforcement. Sent when contact with the other teams is lost. Arbiters finish the collapse where everyone else failed, whatever it costs.

NLOC file 02
SHELL

Power runs through the Shell

The SHELL (Spatial Hybrid Enclave Link Layer) is your base, your backpack and your power source. Every buff you have comes from the pods mounted on it.

  1. 01 · Pods

    Pods grant buffs

    Mount pods on the Pod Array, the 3 by 4 grid on the outside of your Shell. Each pod adds its own buff, from faster sprinting to stronger firepower while in lightning storms. Pods are arranged from inside the Shell while it is deployed.

  2. 02 · Deploy

    Deploy into a Gravity Well

    Deploy the Shell into a Gravity Well and Spatial Hybrid technology expands it into a full size base of operations. Its buffs extend across the Boundary around the Well, and inside that radius you have every pod effect plus health and AUX regeneration.

  3. 03 · Out of bounds

    Past the Boundary, nothing

    Step beyond the Boundary and every buff and all regeneration switch off. The best resources and pod recipes can be found out there.

  4. 04 · Compact

    Or take it with you

    Compact the Shell onto your back and your buffs follow you anywhere on the map. Your Pod Array stays locked while it is worn.

  5. 05 · Risk

    Killed while wearing it

    If an enemy Operator kills you while you wear the Shell, they carry it back to the level’s starting Gravity Well and invades it. You respawn inside to fight them off. Win and you keep everything. Lose and your banked resources and every equipped pod are gone.

  6. 06 · Relocate

    Move to the next Well

    When an area is cleared, compact the Shell, carry it to another Gravity Well and deploy it there. Your Boundary moves with it.

NLOC file 03
ADELLINE suit

Your suit keeps the score

The ADELLINE suit holds your health in batteries, runs your abilities on AUX, and stores Flux for when you need a second skin. Through Link Layer technology, fatal damage triggers a Black Hole Shift that sends you back inside your own Shell to safety.

Battery

1,000 HP each

Health is stacked in battery bands. Lose a band and that battery is gone until you recharge. Find more to raise your ceiling.

AUX

Capped by HP

Energy for abilities and the ammo for most weapons. Its maximum always equals your current health, so taking hits shrinks what you can spend.

Flux

Supercharged

Collected in the field. Inverted at a Field Inverter, it Supercharges your suit with over-health, faster AUX recharge and higher damage output. Pod recipes require it for fabrication. Deposit and withdraw it at your Shell’s reserves to stock up when the Player is preparing for a dangerous excursion.

NLOC file 04
Pod Array

Twelve cells. Make them count.

Pods mount on a 3 by 4 grid on the outside of your Shell. Fitting the pods you want into twelve cells is part of every loadout decision.

Example Pod Array

I II III IV

Color shows tier. Layout is for illustration.

Any shape, any tier

Pods come in single cells, straight bars, L shapes, T shapes, squares and more. Shape and tier are separate, so a small pod can be powerful and a large one can be modest.

Learn the recipe

Reverse engineer a pod you find to learn how it is built. From then on you can craft it whenever you have the resources, and a recipe is never lost.

Arranged from inside

The Pod Array can only be changed from inside your Shell while it is deployed in a Gravity Well. Plan your loadout before you head out.

NLOC file 05
Stakes

Know what you can lose

Anything you carry is at risk. Walk into your Shell and your carried resources bank automatically. What else you lose depends on where your Shell was when you went down.

Case A

Your Shell is deployed

You die anywhere on the map. You return to your Shell and play on.

  • LostEverything you were carrying
  • KeptBanked resources
  • KeptEquipped pods
  • KeptLearned recipes
Case B

An enemy Operator kills you while you wear it

They take your Shell to the starting Gravity Well and invade it. You respawn inside and fight.

You win the fight
  • LostEverything you were carrying
  • KeptBanked resources
  • KeptEquipped pods
  • KeptLearned recipes
You lose the fight
  • LostEverything you were carrying
  • LostBanked resources
  • LostEquipped pods
  • KeptLearned recipes

An equipped Black Box Pod saves one carried slot on any death.

Studio

Via Games

Via Games is an independent studio in New Jersey. ArcDROP is our first title, built in Unreal Engine 5 with C++ systems underneath.

There is one person behind the keyboard here, but calling ArcDROP a solo project would leave a lot of people out. Asset creators, the Unreal Engine community and many knowledgeable people who answered questions along the way have all helped get the game to where it is today.
